Load demo
Opens the built-in 140 BPM drill example and its matching drum stem.
Open audio
Loads a mix or loop for analysis. You can also drop an audio file onto the app.
Add drum stem / Replace stem
Loads an isolated drum track. Export it from the same starting point as the mix so the hits line up.
Original / Stem / Rebuilt
Hear the loaded mix, your supplied drum stem, or the current drum pattern played by Drumagon’s kit.
Detection
Full mix scans the original audio. Drum stem scans your isolated drums. Combined uses hits from both. This decides which hits appear in the grid.

Playback and tempo

▶ PlayLoop BPM 140÷2×2 BAR 1   0.120 s
Play / Pause
Plays or pauses the visible bar range. Space also toggles playback when you’re outside a control.
Loop
Repeats the selected bars until you pause or turn Loop off.
Waveform and time display
Click the waveform or use its slider to move the playhead. The clock shows the current time in the audio.
BPM / ÷2 / ×2
Sets the grid tempo. Use ÷2 or ×2 if a 140 BPM beat is read as 280 or 70. These controls change the grid spacing and export tempo.
Bar 1 / Set at playhead
Enter the first downbeat in seconds, or seek to it in the waveform and press Set at playhead to line up the bars.

‹ / Bars in view / ›
Go back or forward by the selected range. Choose 1, 2, 4, or 8 bars.
Grid and lane names
Select a hit to inspect it. Click an empty spot to seek, or double-click it to add a hit. Tap the lane name to mute or unmute it in Rebuilt playback.
Instrument
Move the selected hit to Kick, Snare / Clap, Closed Hat, or Open Hat. It also sets the lane for Add hit at playhead.
Time (ms) / From nearest step
Time moves the selected hit within the file. The offset tells you how early (−) or late (+) it lands relative to the nearest sixteenth-note grid line.
Velocity
Sets how hard the rebuilt kit plays the hit and its MIDI velocity, from 1 to 127. Taller grid marks have higher velocity.
Add hit at playhead / Delete / Undo edit
Insert a hit at the cursor, remove the selected hit, or reverse the most recent edit for the current detection input.
Bar changes
Shows how many drum steps each bar adds or removes compared with the first bar in view.

Export to FL Studio

Export MIDI ↓→ Selected bars / Whole track→FL Studio Piano Roll
Selected bars
Exports hits in the visible bar range, starting at the first selected bar.
Whole track
Exports every hit in the current grid from the start of the audio, including pickups before bar 1.
Map snare/clap to GM clap
Sends that lane to MIDI note 39 (clap) instead of 38 (snare). Use this when your drum kit expects the clap mapping.
